**Quick glossary (first uses).** 0DTE: option that expires the same day. ORB: opening range breakout (price breaks out of an early range). NBBO: the best bid and ask across exchanges; a "quote". Traded price: only where a trade actually printed; thin 0DTE strikes often have none in a minute. ATR: average true range, the typical size of recent price swings. GEX: gamma exposure, an estimate of how option dealers' hedging pushes price (pinning vs amplifying). CI: confidence interval, the range the true value probably sits in; "sig" means the CI excludes zero, "ns" means it does not. Sharpe: return divided by its wobble. MFE: max favourable excursion, the furthest price travelled in your favour. Sigma: one standard daily move, used as a ruler. OTM: out of the money. Theta: daily time-decay of an option's price. IV/RV: implied vs realised volatility. Delta/gamma: how much the option price moves per $1 of underlying, and how fast that changes. Sign test: counts years that are positive, assumes nothing else. Holdout: data kept aside and only used to check a rule picked on the rest.
