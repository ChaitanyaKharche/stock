Plain-English glossary used below (defined once):
- 0DTE: options that expire the same day.
- bp: basis point, one hundredth of one percent.
- VRP: variance risk premium, the amount option sellers are paid for taking volatility risk.
- QLIKE: a loss score for volatility forecasts; lower is better.
- HAR: a standard simple volatility forecasting model (HAR-RV uses past realised volatility; HAR-IV adds option-implied volatility; HAR-IV-TOD adds time-of-day).
- SSD: second-order stochastic dominance, a rule from a published paper ("Almeida et al.") for deciding when an option is mispriced.
- Slurm / sbatch / srun: the job scheduler on the university cluster (HPC = high-performance computing cluster).
- Holm / SPA: statistical corrections for having tried many things (SPA = Hansen's Superior Predictive Ability test).
- Pre-registration: writing down the test and the pass/fail rule before looking at results.
- Frozen lab: the live forward test whose configuration must never change; its "config hash" detects any change.
