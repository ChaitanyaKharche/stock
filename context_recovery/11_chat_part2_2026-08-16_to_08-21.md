Plain-English glossary used here (defined once):
- **0DTE option**: an option that expires the same day it is bought.
- **ORB / opening-range breakout**: buy when price breaks out of a reference range early in the session.
- **NBBO**: the best bid and best ask across all exchanges; "real NBBO pricing" means buying at the ask and selling at the bid from recorded quotes.
- **Breakeven win rate**: the share of trades that must win for the strategy to make zero money, given how big the average win and average loss are.
- **MFE**: maximum favourable excursion, the best the price got after entry (before it comes back).
- **Lookahead bug**: the test uses information that would not have been known at the moment of the trade.
- **t-statistic (t)**: how many standard errors the result is from zero. Roughly, |t| below 2 means "cannot tell from noise".
- **Design effect**: how much trades on the same day/year being correlated shrinks the real sample size.
- **Pre-registration**: writing down the test and the pass/fail rule before looking at results.
